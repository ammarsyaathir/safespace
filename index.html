01 / Understand the signs

Abuse can look different in every home.

Domestic violence may involve a pattern of power, fear, or control. It is not always visible, and it is never the survivor's fault.

KNOWLEDGE · AWARENESS

Physical abuse

Hitting, pushing, restraining, bodily assault, or threatening physical injury.

Emotional abuse

Humiliation, intimidation, psychological degradation, or behaviour creating fear.

Financial control

Restricting money, denying food or medical needs, or sabotaging employment.

Digital monitoring

Controlling devices, tracking location, reading messages, or cyber-harassment.
