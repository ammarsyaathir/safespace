03 / Pause & prepare

Take a calming breath

A gentle grounding exercise. Follow the expanding circle at your own pace. You can stop or restart at any time if it does not feel comfortable.

YOUR PACE · NO PRESSURE
60-SECOND RESET

One breath at a time.

Relax your shoulders. Breathe in gently as the circle expands, pause briefly, then breathe out as it shrinks. Never force your breathing.

Ready

Ready when you are.

This is an optional grounding activity, not medical advice. If focusing on breathing feels uncomfortable, stop and notice 5 things you can see around you.

IF IT FEELS SAFE

A small safety plan

You do not need to check everything. Consider only what is safe, feasible, and practical for your personal situation.

Important: Do not save or carry written safety plans if finding them could put you at higher risk.
