Crisis Hotlines & Institutional Contacts

Verified emergency numbers, non-governmental shelters, legal aid bodies, and government social welfare divisions.

Talian Kasih 15999

National Ministry hotline (KPWKM) available 24/7 for cases of domestic violence, child protection, and welfare assistance.

Call 15999

One Stop Crisis Centre (OSCC)

Located at the Emergency Department of major government hospitals. Provides integrated medical, legal, and police lodging under one roof.

Available at General Hospitals

Legal Aid Bureau (JBG)

Jabatan Bantuan Guaman provides legal advice, representation, and assistance for Interim Protection Orders (IPO) for eligible individuals.

Legal Advice & Protection

Women's Aid Organisation (WAO)

Shelter, crisis support line, and social work assistance for women and children experiencing domestic violence in Malaysia.

03-3000 8858

All Women's Action Society (AWAM)

Offers free counseling, legal information, and emotional support helplines for gender-based violence survivors.

03-7877 0224

Social Welfare Dept (JKM)

Issuing authority for Emergency Protection Orders (EPO), which take effect immediately within 2 hours without court attendance.
